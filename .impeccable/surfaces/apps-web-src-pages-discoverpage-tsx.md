Operate: a listener searches a personal music service, then plays or saves a result.

Extend the existing Hito browsing workspace and its dark, orange-accented controls. Keep the listening panel and player. Use a compact service selector and search form above a music list with neutral artwork, real title/artist/release data, duration, Play, and Add to library. Do not invent covers, catalogs, rankings, or listening statistics.

One service and one page of results are visible at a time. Switching services or account clears private results. Search, paging, preparation, failure, unavailable audio, and saved states are explicit. A delayed selection cannot replace a newer playback command.

On phones, stack the form and row actions; keep controls above the dock, visible focus, readable text, and no page overflow. Empty states lead to connected-service Settings. The global library search is omitted here so this task has one search form.
