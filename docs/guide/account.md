# Your account

Each person on a Hito server has their own account. Your playlists, likes, listening history and edits belong to you. This page covers your sign-in, your profile, the screen lock and your history.

Most of these settings are in **Settings → Profile** and **Settings → Privacy & lock**. You can also find any setting with the search box in **Settings**.

## Change your password

1. Open **Settings → Profile**.
2. Under **Change password**, select **Change password**.
3. Enter your **Current password**.
4. Enter the **New password** twice. It needs at least 12 characters.
5. Select **Change password**.

Every other device is signed out. The one you are using stays signed in.

After five wrong current passwords, Hito blocks further tries for up to 15 minutes.

## Forgot your password

Hito has no reset email. Ask whoever runs your server for help.

## See where you are signed in

Open **Settings → Profile** and find **Devices signed in**. Each row names the browser and system, such as "Firefox on macOS", and when it was last active. The desktop app shows as "Hito desktop". "This device" marks the one you are using.

## Sign out other devices

In **Devices signed in**:

- Select **Sign out** on one row to end that session.
- Select **Sign out everywhere else** to end every session except this one.

If you see a device you do not recognise, sign it out and change your password.

To sign out of the device you are using, open the account menu and select **Sign out**, or use **Sign out** in **Settings → Profile**.

## Set a display name

Your display name is shown in place of your username in the menu, on Home and in the administrator's account list. You still sign in with your username.

1. Open **Settings → Profile**.
2. Under **Display name**, type a name of up to 64 characters.
3. Select **Save display name**.

Clear the field and save to go back to your username.

## Add a profile photo

1. Open **Settings → Profile**.
2. Under your avatar, select **Change photo** and pick a picture.

Hito accepts JPEG, PNG and WebP files up to 2 MB. It crops the picture to a square and removes hidden details such as location data. To go back to your initials, select **Remove photo**.

Your administrator can see your photo. Other members cannot.

## Lock Hito with a PIN

A PIN locks Hito when you step away, without signing you out. It never replaces your password.

1. Open **Settings → Privacy & lock**.
2. Under **Screen lock**, select **Set a PIN**.
3. Enter **Your password** and a **PIN** of 4 to 8 digits.
4. Select **Save PIN**.

To lock right away, select **Lock now**, or open the account menu and select **Lock**. The music stops and Hito asks for your PIN. Select **Unlock** after typing it.

Five wrong PINs sign that device out. You then need your password to sign in again.

The lock applies to one device at a time. Other devices stay as they are.

## Lock automatically when idle

Under **Screen lock**, set **Lock when idle** to **5 minutes**, **15 minutes** or **1 hour**. The default is **Never**. Only your own keyboard, mouse or touch counts as activity, so Hito still locks while music plays in the background.

## Change or remove your PIN

Under **Screen lock**, select **Change PIN** or **Remove**. Both ask for your password. Removing the PIN unlocks every device signed in to your account.

## Pause your listening history

Hito keeps a list of what you play. It feeds the **History** page and **Recently played** on **Home**.

1. Open **Settings → Privacy & lock**.
2. Under **Listening history**, turn off **Save listening history**.

New plays are no longer added. What is already saved stays visible. Turn the switch on again to resume. Pausing history does not affect where a track or episode resumes from.

## Delete your listening history

1. Open **Settings → Privacy & lock**.
2. Next to **Delete listening history**, select **Delete…**.
3. Select **Delete history**.

This removes every entry from **History** and clears **Recently played**. Likes, playlists, resume positions and downloads stay. It cannot be undone. Deleting does not pause history, so new plays are saved again unless you also turn the switch off.

You cannot delete a single entry or a date range yet.

## What stays private

- Your playlists, likes, listening history and resume positions belong to your account.
- Title, artist and album edits are yours. They never change the files or what others see.
- Plugin connections and any tokens you enter are private to your account. Tokens are stored encrypted. The administrator cannot see them.
- Other members cannot see your account, your photo or your activity.

The administrator can see your username, display name, photo, role and when you were last active. The administrator can also delete your account.

Some features contact services outside your server. You choose them in **Settings → Privacy & lock** under **What Hito sends out**. See [your library](library.md#choose-what-hito-looks-up-online). Sharing with the community service is separate and off until you turn it on in the **Community** card.
