# Podcasts and audiobooks

Hito follows podcasts by their RSS feed and turns your own playlists into audiobooks. Both live in Library, under **Podcasts** and **Audiobooks**. They use the same player as music, so [Listening](listening.md) applies here too.

## Podcasts

### Subscribe to a podcast

You need the show's RSS feed address. Most podcast websites list it, often as "RSS".

1. Open **Library**, then **Podcasts**.
2. Select **Subscribe**.
3. Paste the address into **Feed address** and select **Subscribe**.

Hito checks the feed and lists the show. Its episodes appear on the show's page after the first check. Subscribing downloads no audio. Audio plays only when you choose an episode.

Your subscriptions are private to your account.

### Get new episodes

Hito checks each feed about once an hour. New episodes appear on the show's page after a check.

To check now, open the show and select **Refresh feed**. If a check fails, the show is marked "Last feed check failed" and Hito keeps the episodes it already had.

### Play an episode

Open a show to see its episodes. Each one shows its state:

- **New**, with its length, if you have not started it.
- The time left, if you stopped partway.
- **Played**, once you reach the end.

Select **Play** to start an episode, or **Resume** to continue where you stopped. **Start over** plays it from the beginning. **Play latest** at the top of the page starts the newest episode, and **Continue** picks up the one you were in.

Use **Unplayed** and **All** to filter the list. Hito saves your place as you listen, so progress follows your account to other devices.

### Download an episode

1. Select the episode title to open its page.
2. Go to the **Sources** tab and select **Download**.

The page shows progress, then **Saved on this server**. A saved episode plays from your Hito server, even if the feed goes away. To delete the copy, select **Remove download**.

Refreshing a feed or playing an episode never downloads it. Only **Download** does.

### Unsubscribe

Open the show and select **Unsubscribe**. Hito stops checking the feed and the show leaves your list.

Your likes, progress and playlist entries for its episodes are kept. Episodes you downloaded stay playable. Episodes you did not download stop playing, because Hito no longer reads the feed.

To follow the show again, subscribe with the same address.

## Audiobooks

### Make an audiobook

An audiobook in Hito is a playlist you own, read as chapters. Each track in the playlist is one chapter, in playlist order.

1. Add the audiobook's files to a music folder. See [Library](library.md).
2. Create a playlist and add the files in chapter order. See [Playlists](playlists.md).
3. Open **Library**, then **Audiobooks**.
4. Select **Add audiobook from a playlist**, choose the playlist and confirm.

The book appears in Audiobooks. It keeps the playlist's name and order. A followed playlist cannot become an audiobook, but an editable copy of one can.

### Play chapters

Open a book to see **Chapters**. Select a chapter to play it from the beginning. The chapter you are in shows "Listening" and your position.

To change the chapter order, edit the playlist. The book follows it.

### Continue where you stopped

Once you have listened, the book shows **Continue**, with the chapter number and time. Select it to resume that chapter at your saved position and speed.

### Add a bookmark

1. Open the book and select **Bookmark**.
2. Choose a **Chapter**.
3. Enter the **Position in seconds** and a **Bookmark label**.
4. Select **Save bookmark**.

Bookmarks are private to your account. Select **Play bookmark** to jump to one, or **Remove bookmark** to delete it. If a chapter is removed from the playlist, its bookmarks stay and show "Chapter unavailable".

### Change the speed

Use **Speed** in the player or in Now playing, from 0.5× to 2×. Hito remembers the speed with your progress, so **Continue** resumes at the same speed.

Speed is not available in the desktop app yet. See [Apps](apps.md).
